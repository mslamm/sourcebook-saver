Sourcebook

Sourcebook is a citation helper for Google Docs, with a Chrome extension (Sourcebook Saver) that saves articles to the student's own Google Drive in one click.

It is used only inside the school's Google Workspace. Students don't install anything: the school's IT installs it for them.
